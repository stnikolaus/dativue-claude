# Dativue starter prompts

- Find the strongest current content trends about my topic across Reddit, X, LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube. Cite every source, and estimate credits only if I ask.
- Analyze a competitor's recent public social posts. Show the patterns behind engagement and five differentiated content opportunities.
- Mine the comments on these public posts for repeated questions, objections, desires, and exact audience language.
- Transcribe this public video and turn it into a short-video outline, LinkedIn post outline, and email outline without inventing details.
- Find keyword opportunities for my topic with measured volume, difficulty, and intent.
- Compare these topics over the past six months on Google Web and YouTube.
- Analyze the current Google results for this query and cite every ranking page.
- Find topics with growing search demand, then validate them against current social conversations.
- Investigate a niche for underserved customer problems, competing solutions and payment evidence. Quote a plan within 50 credits and recommend a demand test before building.
- Find why these competitors outrank my site, including keyword gaps, top pages, and backlink opportunities.
- Compare how ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google AI results talk about my brand.
- Analyze public reviews to find repeated complaints, requested improvements, and strengths.
- Compare products, apps, or local businesses using current public listings, reviews, and demand.
- Audit this public website and prioritize technical SEO, performance, content, and accessibility issues.

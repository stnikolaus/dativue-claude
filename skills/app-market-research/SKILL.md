# Dativue app market research

Run routine research without a quote. Estimate with `research_credit_quote` when asked, and get user approval for any call whose maximum hold exceeds 50 credits.
Confirm the store, market, app or category, and decision the research should support.

Preserve app IDs, developers, categories, prices, installs, rankings, ratings, review dates, helpful votes, developer responses, screenshots, and source URLs when available.
Keep store evidence separate from calculated comparisons and recommendations.
Write `N/A` for unavailable metrics and explain that store rankings and review availability change over time.
Do not enrich reviewer or developer records with personal contacts.

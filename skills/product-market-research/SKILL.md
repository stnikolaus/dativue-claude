# Dativue product market research

Run routine research without a quote. Estimate with `research_credit_quote` when asked, and get user approval for any call whose maximum hold exceeds 50 credits.
Confirm the marketplace, market, product or category, and decision.

Preserve product IDs, URLs, prices, sellers, specifications, variants, images, shipping, ratings, sponsored or organic placement, competitors, ranked keywords, and demand when available.
Keep retrieved product data separate from calculated comparisons and conclusions.
Write `N/A` for missing fields and explain that prices, availability, placements, and seller details can change quickly.
State that Amazon review retrieval is unavailable until the provider restores it.
Do not enrich products or sellers with personal contact details or infer private information about individuals.

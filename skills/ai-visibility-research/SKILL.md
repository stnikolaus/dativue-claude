# Dativue AI visibility research

Run routine research without a quote. Estimate with `research_credit_quote` when asked, and get user approval for any call whose maximum hold exceeds 50 credits.
Use AI visibility data for measured mentions, cited sources, brands, pages, competitors, and history.
Use AI response benchmarks only for supplied visibility questions, not as a general-purpose answering tool.

Report each model, prompt, response date, brand mention, citation, and cited domain when available.
Clearly label AI demand inferred from People Also Ask or other search behavior rather than direct AI-query logs.
Keep provider evidence, cross-model calculations, and conclusions separate.
Write `N/A` for missing values and explain that generated answers can change between runs.
Do not enrich results with personal contact details or infer private information about people mentioned in responses.

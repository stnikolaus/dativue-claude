# Dativue local market research

Run routine research without a quote. Estimate with `research_credit_quote` when asked, and get user approval for any call whose maximum hold exceeds 50 credits.
Confirm the category and location, and use coordinates only when the user provides or approves them.

Preserve public business names, categories, addresses, coordinates, websites, business phones, hours, attributes, services, ratings, reviews, updates, questions, images, and source URLs when available.
Separate observed business data, calculated market comparisons, and conclusions.
Write `N/A` for missing fields and explain that profiles may be incomplete or outdated.
Do not retrieve or infer personal contact information.

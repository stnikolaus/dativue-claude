# Dativue competitor search intelligence

Run routine research without a quote. Estimate with `research_credit_quote` when asked, and get user approval for any call whose maximum hold exceeds 50 credits.
Use `search_competitor_visibility` for measured rankings, traffic estimates, pages, competitors, and movement.
Use `search_keyword_gap` only when the comparison needs shared, missing, unique, or weak keywords across two to five domains.

Preserve provider metrics, dates, ranks, and source URLs.
Separate retrieved evidence, calculations, and recommendations.
Write `N/A` for unavailable fields and explain that traffic and value figures are estimates.
Do not infer personal contacts or people behind a domain.

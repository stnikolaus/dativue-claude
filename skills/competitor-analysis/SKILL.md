# Competitor content analysis

Use this workflow to understand what a named competitor publishes, what earns engagement, and where the user can differentiate.

1. Confirm the competitor and target networks. Work only with public company/profile/channel targets; do not search for employees, emails, or private data.
2. Retrieve the relevant public profiles and posts directly. Use `research_credit_quote` when the user asks for an estimate or the plan may exceed 50 credits, and get approval for a higher-cost plan.
3. Use `social_profile` when identity or account matching is uncertain; otherwise use `social_posts` for up to 20 recent posts per network.
4. Compare recurring subjects, hooks, formats, publishing cadence, calls to action, and engagement relative to the competitor’s own baseline. Do not compare raw engagement across networks as though their metrics are equivalent.
5. Cite representative original posts and identify the observed evidence behind each conclusion.
6. End with five content opportunities that are meaningfully different from the competitor rather than copies of their best posts.

Default maximum: 23 credits for one `social_posts` call on all six networks at pricing version 2026-08-31.

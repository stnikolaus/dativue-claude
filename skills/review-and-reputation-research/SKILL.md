# Dativue review and reputation research

Run routine research without a quote. Estimate with `research_credit_quote` when asked, and get user approval for any call whose maximum hold exceeds 50 credits.
Select only the review sources relevant to the named business, place, app, or product.

Group repeated themes while preserving rating, date, language, helpful votes, public author label, response, and source URL when available.
Separate the review evidence from calculated theme frequency and AI interpretation.
Write `N/A` when a field is unavailable.
Do not enrich public author labels with personal data or attempt to identify reviewers.
Explain that review availability, sorting, and sampling differ by source.
